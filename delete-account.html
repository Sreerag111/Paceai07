Account and Data Deletion Policy for PaceAI

App Name: PaceAI • Developer: Paceaitechnologies (OPC) Private Limited • Website: https://paceai.in • Support Email: paceai.support@gmail.com

At PaceAI, we give our users full control over their personal data. In compliance with Google Play Store policies, you have the right to request the permanent deletion of your account and all associated personal information.

How to Request Account and Data Deletion

You can request the deletion of your PaceAI account and personal data at any time without needing to keep the app installed. Please follow these simple steps:

1. Send an Email:

From the email address registered with your PaceAI account, send an email to: paceai.support@gmail.com

2. Subject Line:

Account Deletion Request - PaceAI

3. Required Information in Email Body:
  • Your registered Email Address (e.g., your Google Sign-In or email account)
  • Your full name as displayed in the app
  • A brief statement: "I request the permanent deletion of my PaceAI account and all associated personal data."

What Data Will Be Permanently Deleted

Upon receiving and verifying your request, we permanently purge the following data from our servers and databases:

  • Account & Profile Information: Your name, email address, user ID, profile photo, and authentication credentials.
  • Chat & Prompt History: All conversation logs, chat history, and prompt interactions.
  • Media & Generated Content: All images created, photos enhanced, videos processed, and temporary files stored in cloud storage.
  • App Usage Data: Any user-specific preferences and diagnostic records tied to your account.

What Data May Be Retained & Why

In accordance with applicable law and Google Play policy, certain limited records cannot be deleted immediately:

  • Financial & Transaction Records: Information regarding subscription payments, pack purchases, and GST/tax invoices processed through our payment gateway (Razorpay) will be retained for the minimum period required by Indian financial and tax regulations for fraud prevention and legal compliance.
  • No personal chat or media data is retained after deletion.

Processing Timeframe

  • Your deletion request will be processed and verified within 48–72 hours.
  • All account and associated personal data will be permanently and irreversibly deleted within 30 days of verification.
  • You will receive a confirmation email once your account has been completely removed.
